## Why this is materially distinct from earlier reports

Recovery471 documents a **second accepted NEXT after pending** reaching `ordinary_next_candidate` and potentially mutating Side/postponement state before the lower-level pending guard. This audit locates another code-level fail-open: exiting ordinary management entirely and returning via normal PStartMenu `0x323` without requiring source match completion. It also shows why implementing only a repeat-NEXT button guard would be insufficient: the original current modal/result owner must gate **every** ordinary management pointer/nav/Return action for this phase.
