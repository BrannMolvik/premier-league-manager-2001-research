## Correct scope and direct consequences

- **CONFIRMED code-path defect:** ordinary NEXT never calls the source-backed country daily updater through this path, even though the whole/general and both separate fixture-aware day APIs do. All new games initialize country gate values `True` (`GameState.from_database` around line468). When the ordinary career crosses the source-proven England boundary **30 March 2001**, the country gate can remain `True` instead of flipping closed, unless some **other independently proven call path** updates it. There is **no such hook in the traced ordinary NEXT path**. At the 31 May 2001 reopening date the missing second toggle likewise prevents source-equivalent lifecycle; a stale always-open gate could let AI acquisition proceed in a closed period. This conditional career divergence matters well beyond Gate13 pixel fidelity.
- **CONFIRMED omitted execution, CONDITIONAL material effects:** commercial timing/offer mechanics also do not run during these ordinary NEXT days **when `user_commercial_timers` is populated**, and source-backed daily training recovery/Saturday training does not run **when training threshold and quality are explicitly configured**. Missing/null settings are intentionally fail-closed and consume no RNG, so do not claim that every fresh game necessarily loses training points, RNG draws or an actual sponsor deal.
- **Source ordering cannot be repaired by copy/paste of `GameState.advance_one_day`**: the ordinary method has already incremented the date, is tracking mutable linked/Side event authority and suspends at a human pre-match modal. Duplicating the full general method would advance another day, invalidate original retained wrapper proofs or replay after pending; avoid that. The fix must integrate **qualified maintenance phases into exactly one original date**, preserve commercial-before-training, country toggle-before-Saturday acquisition, and postpone day-end maintenance until after the accepted human fixture without rerunning it.
- **Not established here:** native exact placement of *every* commercial/training event inside multi-manager original PResults two-container walks, real England AI purchase after the March boundary, special country calendars, source timing for an incomplete pending fixture, and full Windows11 playability. These require first-party source runtime/Windows acceptance. This is not an observed transfer-window cheat or a basis to invent new UI.
