## Recovery628: independently reached source player pointer/ID and raw owner validation

Static disassembly of the same verified original private PE (SHA256 `833bf95e92a1c76ade47106f8ad7d3ca307069b7e5778a7067cd0658838b7cc3`) confirmed the **actual** source control flow at 0x5127AF–0x512932, not execution. The 0x512853/0x5128FC loops load unsigned player IDs from each source side+0x244 and dynamically resolve them through the 0x875644 object's first vtable entry, then call 0x419490 or 0x419680. The active rows at 0x5128A7 instead load an already-resolved player pointer from the 0x5127A0 processing owner+0x04 (first side) or +0x5B4 (second). Native `player+4` gives an independently reached unsigned player identity. The pointer getter is a virtual callback: do **not** manufacture a numeric `base + 592 × id` pointer in order to claim its result.
