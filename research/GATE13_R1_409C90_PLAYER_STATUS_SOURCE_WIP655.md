## Exact native source bit stores recovered so far

- Native **4EA330** reads status BYTE+3 and the low caller argument BYTE; XOR/AND/XOR results in `new_status[3] = (old_status[3] & 0xE0) | (source_argument & 0x1F)`. It preserves high three status bits.
- Native **4EA350** independently reads status BYTE+4 and another low caller argument BYTE; result `new_status[4] = (old_status[4] & 0xF0) | (source_argument & 0x0F)`. It preserves high four bits. The two original calls are ordered: +3 before +4.
- Native **4182F0** first executes `OR DWORD [player+0x14],0x10` at `4182F3` before separate 418160,418180,418170,417340 and conditional 41EDF0 callbacks. Thus the first direct bit4 write can be projected, but it is **not proof the entire 4182F0 or player/world mutation completed**. 4181B0 for clearing/other selection also calls `4EA370` and other native state mutators; unresolved.
