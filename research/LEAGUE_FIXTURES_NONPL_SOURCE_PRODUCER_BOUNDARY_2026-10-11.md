## Correction: a non-PL event token is **not** the current club's League identity

Recovery485's example characterizes `('league_match',27,0,18)` as **Southport349's League node**. That attribution is not supported by the cited probe. `research/GATE13_NEXT_EVENT_LIFECYCLE_2026-10-10.md` says a Southport Conference fixture was pending on **19 August**, and separately lists a `('cup_result',9,200,0)` and `('league_match',27,0,18)` Side conflict on **23 August**. It does **not** establish that the latter node involves Southport or gives club349 its membership ID.

Crucial countercheck: `reconstruction/competition_materializer.py` at its competition-ID-27 branch generates original-type-4 **Scottish Premier split** LeagueMatch emissions, and `reconstruction/competition_schedule.py::materialize_scot_premier_split_schedule_nodes` explicitly handles those type-4 Scottish split refs with competition27. This does not on its own prove the precise numeric **Conference** League identity, nor does it prove what the live original would display for club349. It does invalidate treating the unrelated event's `27` as a source-verified club349 membership value.

**Correct acceptance source:** query the actual canonical `GameState.club_competition_membership[349]` **for the selected Southport career** (and the actual `state.clubs[349].country_id`), then verify it against original `DBRClub+0x10/+0x14` evidence. Do not infer membership from arbitrary contemporaneous global match events, and do not hardcode 27 in a Southport-facing selector test. Negative: a match involving another club in the same day must not change this manager's default panel League.
