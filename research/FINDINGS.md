# Confirmed Findings

This document contains technical findings that have been verified strongly enough to reuse in later sessions.

## Standard for adding a finding

A finding should include:
- what was observed;
- how it was verified;
- relevant file names, offsets, addresses, symbols, APIs, or commands;
- confidence level if uncertainty remains;
- implications for the next investigation step.

Do not promote guesses or untested hypotheses to confirmed findings.
